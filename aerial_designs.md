# Aerial Designs

- **Fixed-pitch (low-Re)** — Moulded or carbon blade cut for one rpm; measured data is public for most common sizes.  [UIUC propeller database](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/propDB.html) · **CAD:** [PropFolio OpenVSP models](https://propfolio.live/)
- **Custom carbon blade** — Planform and pitch made to order for one airframe and motor pairing.  [Mejzlik](https://www.mejzlik.eu/) · **CAD:** [propeller-FreeCAD](https://github.com/Rigel-Alves/propeller-FreeCAD)
  **Collective pitch** — Blade pitch actuated in flight, so thrust changes without changing rpm.  [T-Motor VP props](https://store.tmotor.com/categorys/manual-variable-pitch-polymer-propeller) · **CAD:** [Variable-pitch propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/variable-pitch-reverse-thrust-propeller)
- **eVTOL proprotor** — Rigid blade with no cyclic, sized to work in hover and in wing-borne cruise across a tilt conversion.  [Tilt-wing proprotor optimisation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1270963823007319) · **CAD:** [Tiltrotor UAV](https://grabcad.com/library/tiltrotor-uav-1)
- **Folding blade** — Blades fold back when unpowered to cut drag in winged cruise.  [T-Motor folding props](https://store.tmotor.com/categorys/folding-carbon-fiber-propeller) · **CAD:** [Folding propellers (STL)](https://www.printables.com/model/89982-folding-propellers-for-rocket-drones)
- **Ducted fan** — Rotor in a close-fitting duct; high static thrust for the disc area, though the duct's gain fades with forward speed and costs weight and wetted area.  [Schübeler](https://www.schubeler.com/home/) · **CAD:** [EDF unit, 5 blade](https://grabcad.com/library/edf-ducted-fan-unit-2-5inch-5-blade)
- **Coaxial contra-rotating** — Two contra-rotating rotors on one axis; cancels torque and packs two discs into one footprint, but the lower rotor works in the upper's wake and the pair falls well short of double the thrust.  [Coaxial swashplateless UAV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04251) · **CAD:** [Coaxial propeller V2](https://grabcad.com/library/coaxial-propeller-v2-1)
- **Tip device (Q-tip)** — Tip curled aft as an inverted winglet; the certified, conservative way to break up the tip vortex.  [Hartzell Q-Tip](https://hartzellprop.com/products/top-prop/piper/twin-comanche-2-blade-q-tip/) · **CAD:** [Swept profile blade](https://grabcad.com/library/swept-profile-propeller-blade)
- **Toroidal** — Looped blade with no tip; redistributes tonal noise rather than reliably removing it, and measured examples pay for it in torque.  [MIT Lincoln Laboratory](https://www.ll.mit.edu/partner-us/available-technologies/toroidal-propeller) · [Measured against conventional blades](https://acta-acustica.edpsciences.org/articles/aacus/full_html/2026/01/aacus260084/aacus260084.html) · **CAD:** [Toroidal propeller generator](https://github.com/RaulBejarano/Ultimate-Toroidal-Propeller-Generator)
- **Serrated edge** — Owl-inspired saw-tooth leading and trailing edges that break up edge noise without changing the planform.  [Serrated TE for drone noise](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0003682X25005171)
- **Cyclorotor** — Vertical blades on a drum with cyclic pitch; instant thrust vectoring at low tip speed.  [Hover performance study](https://sites.utexas.edu/sirohi/files/2017/07/010_parsons07_jahs.pdf)
