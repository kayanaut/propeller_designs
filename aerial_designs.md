# Aerial Designs

- **Fixed-pitch (low-Re)** — Moulded or carbon blade cut for one rpm; measured data is public for most common sizes.  [UIUC propeller database](https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/propDB.html) · **CAD:** [PropFolio OpenVSP models](https://propfolio.live/)
- **Custom carbon blade** — Planform and pitch made to order for one airframe and motor pairing.  [Mejzlik](https://www.mejzlik.eu/) · **CAD:** [propeller-FreeCAD](https://github.com/Rigel-Alves/propeller-FreeCAD)
  **Collective pitch** — Blade pitch actuated in flight, so thrust changes without changing rpm.  [T-Motor VP props](https://store.tmotor.com/categorys/manual-variable-pitch-polymer-propeller) · **CAD:** [Variable-pitch propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/variable-pitch-reverse-thrust-propeller)
- **Folding blade** — Blades fold back when unpowered to cut drag in winged cruise.  [T-Motor MFVP](https://store.tmotor.com/product/mf2614vp-variable-pitch-propeller-for-uavs.html) · **CAD:** [Folding propellers (STL)](https://www.printables.com/model/89982-folding-propellers-for-rocket-drones)
- **Ducted fan**— Rotor in a close-fitting duct; high static thrust for the disc area, heavier and speed-limited.  [Schübeler](https://www.schubeler.com/home/) · **CAD:** [EDF unit, 5 blade](https://grabcad.com/library/edf-ducted-fan-unit-2-5inch-5-blade)
- **Coaxial contra-rotating** — Two counter-rotating rotors on one axis; cancels torque and doubles thrust per footprint.  [Coaxial swashplateless UAV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04251) · **CAD:** [Coaxial propeller V2](https://grabcad.com/library/coaxial-propeller-v2-1)
- **Toroidal** — Looped blade with no tip; clearly quieter than a conventional blade at similar thrust.  [MIT Lincoln Laboratory](https://www.ll.mit.edu/partner-us/available-technologies/toroidal-propeller) · **CAD:** [Toroidal propeller generator](https://github.com/RaulBejarano/Ultimate-Toroidal-Propeller-Generator)
- **Cyclorotor** — Vertical blades on a drum with cyclic pitch; instant thrust vectoring at low tip speed.  [Hover performance study](https://sites.utexas.edu/sirohi/files/2017/07/010_parsons07_jahs.pdf)
