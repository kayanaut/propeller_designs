# Underwater Designs

- **Fixed-pitch (FPP)** — Solid cast blade at one pitch; the marine baseline, defined by standard measured series like Wageningen B.  [Wageningen B-Series geometry](https://www.caeses.com/blog/2017/geometry-for-the-design-of-wageningen-b-series-propellers/) · **CAD:** [B-Series propeller generator (STL/STEP)](https://www.wageningen-b-series-propeller.com/)
- **Controllable-pitch (CPP)** — Hub mechanism rotates the blades in service, so thrust and reverse are set without reversing shaft rpm.  [Wärtsilä CPP systems](https://www.wartsila.com/marine/products/propulsors-and-gears/propellers/wartsila-controllable-pitch-propeller-systems) · **CAD:** [Controllable pitch propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/controllable-pitch-propeller-1)
- **Tip-loaded (Kappel / CLT)** — Blade tip bent into an end plate — to the suction side on a Kappel, the pressure side on a CLT — to hold loading out at the tip instead of spilling it over.  [Tip rake on Kappel propellers](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1312/11/4/748)
- **Ducted propeller / Kort nozzle** — Propeller shrouded in a fixed nozzle; boosts bollard-pull thrust on heavily loaded, low-speed hulls like tugs and trawlers, and turns into drag once speed rises.  [Kort nozzle](https://www.wartsila.com/encyclopedia/term/kort-nozzle) · **CAD:** [Tuyère Kort nozzle](https://grabcad.com/library/tuyere-kort-kort-nozzle-1)
  **Pump-jet** — Rotor and stator fully enclosed in a long shroud; suppresses tip-vortex cavitation for quiet running on submarines and torpedoes.  [Pump-jets vs. propellers](https://www.twz.com/31708/veteran-sonarman-explains-why-pump-jets-are-superior-to-props-on-modern-submarines) · **CAD:** [Propeller for ROV thruster](https://grabcad.com/library/propeller-for-rov-thruster-1)
- **Rim-driven (hubless)** — Blades carried on a motor-rotor ring with no hub and no shaft; the ring runs inside a stator built into the duct.  [SCHOTTEL RimThruster](https://www.schottel.de/en/portfolio/products/product-details/srt-schottel-rimthruster) · **CAD:** [Hubless rim-driven propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/hub-less-rim-driven-propeller-1)
- **Contra-rotating (CRP)** — Two coaxial propellers spinning opposite ways recover the swirl energy a single screw sheds into its wake.  [Brunvoll CRP systems](https://www.brunvoll.no/products/crp-contra-rotating-propellers) · **CAD:** [Contra rotating propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/contra-rotating-propeller-1)
- **Surface-piercing (SPP)** — Cleaver-shaped blades run half out of the water, ventilating instead of cavitating; cuts wetted drag on planing hulls.  [Arneson surface drives](https://twindisc.com/arneson/)
- **Supercavitating** — Wedge sections force a vapour cavity off the leading edge that closes behind the blade, so the collapse never touches metal.  [Supercavitating propellers](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/systems/ship/systems/propellers-supercavitating.htm)
- **Skewed blade** — Blades swept back along the disc; delays and quiets cavitation, the standard choice for low-noise submarine and merchant-ship screws.  [Skew and tonal noise](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0029801823006029) · **CAD:** [PropCad (skew/rake parametric)](https://www.hydrocompinc.com/solutions/propcad/)
- **Loop / toroidal (Sharrow)** — Each blade closes into a loop with no free tip; strong gains through the mid range that converge with a well-matched conventional prop at wide-open throttle.  [Sharrow Marine](https://www.sharrowmarine.com/prop) · **CAD:** [Marine toroidal blades](https://grabcad.com/library/e-foil-and-marine-toroidal-propeller-blades-1)
- **Voith Schneider (cycloidal)** — Vertical blades on a rotating disc with cyclic pitch; thrust in any direction with no rudder, used on tugs and ferries.  [Voith-Schneider Propulsor](https://www.wartsila.com/encyclopedia/term/voith-schneider-propulsor-(vsp)-cycloidal-propeller) · **CAD:** [VSP cycloidal rotor pitch control](https://grabcad.com/tutorials/voith-schneider-propeller-cycloidal-rotor-pitch-control-catia-dmu-kinematics-tutorial)
- **Podded azimuth thruster** — Motor sits in a submerged steerable pod driving a fixed-pitch propeller directly; gearless, and steers through 360° instead of using a rudder.  [ABB Azipod](https://new.abb.com/marine/systems-and-solutions/azipod) · **CAD:** [Azimuth pod propeller](https://grabcad.com/library/azimuth-pod-propeller-1)
