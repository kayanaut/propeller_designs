  Regenerated by [`cad/tools/make-geometry.py`](../../tools/make-geometry.py).
  Feeds: the symmetric 16%-thick hydrofoil, pitch axis at 30% chord.
  Source: NACA four-digit thickness equation, Abbott & von Doenhoff, *Theory of Wing Sections* (Dover, 1959), Sec. 6.4. Generated 2026-09-10 at 81 cosine-spaced stations, open trailing edge (`-0.1015 x⁴`). Verified: `y_t(0) = 0`, max thickness 0.160·c to 3e-7, maximum at `x/c = 0.309`. The equation is in the CSV header, so this is re-derivable rather than trusted.
  Without it: nothing — this one is generated. Everything else in the brief (orbit Ø, `N = 5`, span, chord, eccentricity) is a parameter, and the pitch law is defined by the steering-centre construction, so this design is not blocked on any external data.
